🏎️ PRIMARY SPONSORS — CAR LIVERY
Primary sponsors feature prominently on your car. They pay a race bonus for strong finishes and a larger season-end bonus based on your final championship position. Long-term contracts unlock better terms as your team proves itself.
AVAILABLE PRIMARY SPONSORS
👤 SECONDARY SPONSORS — DRIVER SUIT & HELMET
Secondary sponsors are tied to individual drivers — their following determines which sponsors are available. A larger fanbase unlocks better-paying deals. These pay lower than primary but reward driver performance.
🔷 ALTERNATE SPONSORS — MISCELLANEOUS
Alternate sponsors are smaller, short-term deals — paddock hoardings, hospitality branding, pit lane gear. Lower pay, no performance incentives, but quick to sign and easy to renew.
